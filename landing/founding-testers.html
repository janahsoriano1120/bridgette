You can take this back

You can withdraw your consent at any time by emailing dpo@bridgette.com.ph. You do not have to give a reason.

Withdrawing means we can no longer run Bridgette for you, so we will send you a complete export of your health data first, then close the account and delete the data. Your health story is yours, and it comes back to you before it goes anywhere. Leaving this way ends the Founding Tester rate, the same as cancelling.

Withdrawing does not undo processing that already happened while your consent was in place.

You also have the right to complain to the National Privacy Commission at privacy.gov.ph.
