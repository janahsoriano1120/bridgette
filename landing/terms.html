6. Data Sharing and Provider Access

When you share your health data with a provider through Bridgette:

  • You grant the provider access to the data categories you select, on a view-only basis, through a secure share link.

  • Share links have an expiration date that you set, and you can revoke access at any time.

  • Before viewing, the provider enters their full name and profession, and their PRC license number if they are a licensed provider. We record which provider opened your link and when.

  • A provider may write a note back to you. The note is added to your medical history only if you accept it. If you decline it, it is kept in your private archive.

  • Bridgette does not verify the identity of providers, and notes are labeled accordingly. If a provider tells us that they did not write a note shown under their name, we record the dispute, remove their name from the note, and mark it as disputed.

  • Providers may retain their own clinical notes from consultations independent of Bridgette.

The collection, use, disclosure, retention, and other processing of personal data in connection with provider access are governed by Bridgette's Privacy Notice, which forms an integral part of these Terms.

7. Bookings and Messages

Some providers share a Bridgette booking link. Booking with a provider does not give that provider access to your health records. You decide separately what to share, through a share link.

Bookings are currently free. Bridgette does not collect, hold, or process payments for consultations. If a provider charges a fee for a consultation, you and the provider arrange payment directly, outside Bridgette.

You can message providers you have booked with or connected with. Messages can include text, photos, and files. You are responsible for what you send. If a provider sends you a link to a video call on another service, such as Google Meet or WhatsApp, that call takes place on that service under that service's own terms, and it is not recorded by or sent through Bridgette.

Bridgette is a platform for recording and sharing health information. The consultation itself, and the clinical care a provider gives you, is between you and that provider. Bridgette is not a party to it and is not responsible for it.

8. Caregivers

You may give a caregiver, such as a family member, access to your records. By default a caregiver can only view your records. You may allow more, such as sharing with providers you have already approved, or messaging your providers on your behalf.

You choose who you grant access to and what they can do. You can change or remove a caregiver's access at any time. Messages a caregiver sends are clearly labeled as coming from the caregiver, not from you.

9. AI Features

Bridgette uses AI to help you organize your health information. This includes reading the values from laboratory result and consultation photos you choose to add, so that you do not have to type them in, and generating summaries of the logs you record.

We ask for your consent before a photo is read. You can decline and enter the values yourself.

AI output in Bridgette is organizational, not clinical. It does not diagnose, treat, or give medical advice, and it is not a substitute for your doctor. AI can make mistakes, so please check any values that were read from a photo before you rely on them, and raise anything that looks wrong with your provider.
