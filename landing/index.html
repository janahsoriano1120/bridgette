The problem

Your health data is
everywhere except
with you.

Every Filipino patient has the same story. Labs in a folder. Records at three different hospitals. A WhatsApp chat with your last doctor. And every new consultation starts from zero.

Lab results lost

Paper envelopes, WhatsApp forwards, hospital filing cabinets you'll never see again.

Repeating your story

New doctor, new clinic, same questions. Your history disappears the moment you leave.

No trend visibility

Is your sugar improving? Worsening? No one can tell from a single result on a single page.

OFW families cut off

Your nanay is abroad. She has no way to know if you followed up on that lab result.
