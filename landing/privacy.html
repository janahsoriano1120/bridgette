Privacy Notice

Bridgette is built on a simple belief: your health data belongs to you. This Notice explains exactly how we collect, use, protect, and never sell your personal and health information.

2. What Data We Process

Account Information

Patients and healthcare providers may both be data subjects under this Privacy Notice in relation to their own personal data.

When you create an account, we collect your full name, email address, date of birth, biological sex, and role (patient or healthcare provider), and a system-generated User ID. For providers, we may also collect your profession, specialty and subspecialty, telemedicine availability, and clinic, hospital, or other practice affiliations, and, for licensed providers, your PRC license number. Providers who are not licensed by the Professional Regulation Commission (PRC), such as personal trainers and health coaches, are not asked for a PRC license number and are shown to patients as not licensed. Caregivers create their own account with their name and email address, and see only what the patient allows.

Health Data

When you use Bridgette, you may enter information about your medical history, self-reported diagnoses, consultation history, and approximate date of your last doctor visit. You may also record the names, dosages, and schedules of medicines and supplements you take, and upload images of laboratory results. These images may contain personal information, including your name, test results, clinic details, and dates. You control the information you choose to provide through the platform. You may also attach supporting documents to notes you accept from providers, such as a photo of a prescription, receipt, or appointment confirmation. Health data is sensitive personal information, and we process it based on your consent.

Share Links and Provider Notes

When you share your record through a share link, we record which provider opened it and when. Before viewing, the provider enters their full name and profession, and their PRC license number if they are a licensed provider. Providers who open a share link are data subjects under this Privacy Notice in relation to this information, whether or not they have a Bridgette account.

A provider may write a note back to you, which may include a diagnosis if the provider is licensed. We store the note, any diagnosis, the provider’s name, profession, and PRC license number if licensed, and the date and time it was written. The note is added to your medical history only if you accept it, and to accept it you must confirm that the provider wrote it. If you decline it, it is kept in your private archive. Bridgette does not verify the identity of providers, and notes are labeled accordingly. If a provider tells us that they did not write a note shown under their name, we record the dispute, remove their name from the note, and mark it as disputed.

Notes Sent to Individuals Without a Bridgette Account

If a provider sends a note to someone who does not have a Bridgette account, we receive that person’s name and email address from the provider, together with the note. We send a secure link by email only. The email does not contain the note or any health information. The recipient confirms their identity with a one-time code and sees this Privacy Notice before the note opens. If the note is not opened and saved to a Bridgette account within ninety (90) days, it is deleted.

Photos You Add to Your Records

When you choose to add a photo of a laboratory result or a consultation document to your health records, we send the photo through our secure server to Anthropic, our AI processing partner, so that the values can be read and filled in for you. The photo itself may show your name, birthday, or other personal details, and these cannot be removed before the photo is read. We instruct the AI service not to include them in its answer, and we do not send the photo for any other purpose. Photos you send only in a chat with a provider are not sent to Anthropic. We ask for your consent before a photo is read.

Bookings

Some providers, such as guests of Bridgette Health Talks, share a Bridgette booking link. To use a booking link, you create a Bridgette account and are taken to the provider’s page to book. We collect the name and contact details you gave when you signed up, the provider you booked with, and the details of your booking. For now, bookings are free. Bridgette does not collect or hold payments for consultations: if the provider charges a fee, you and the provider arrange payment directly, outside Bridgette. Booking does not give the provider access to your health records. You decide what to share.

Messages

You can message providers you have booked with or connected with in Bridgette. Messages can include text, photos, and files. A message is visible to you, to the provider you sent it to, and to a caregiver only if you have given that caregiver permission to see or send messages. If a provider sends a link to a video call on another service, such as Google Meet or WhatsApp, that call takes place on that service and is not recorded by or sent through Bridgette. Messaging a provider does not share your health records with them. You share records separately, through a share link.

Usage Data

We collect basic analytics about how you use the app to improve the service, including device type, app version, feature usage patterns, and crash logs. We do not track your browsing activity outside of Bridgette.

We use usage data only to run and improve Bridgette: to fix errors and crashes, to keep the app fast and reliable, to see which features are used so we can improve them, to keep Bridgette secure and detect misuse (such as attempts to open share links using another person’s name or license number), and to help you when you contact us for support. We may also prepare aggregated statistics that do not identify any individual, such as the total number of active users, to report on how Bridgette is doing.

Usage data does not include the content of your health records. We do not sell usage data, use it for advertising, share it with advertisers, or use it to build individual profiles for marketing.

Invitation and Referral Data

If you invite someone to Bridgette, your name and User ID may be included in the invitation message generated through the messaging application you use. Bridgette does not receive the invitee's contact details through this invitation flow. If an individual signs up using a referral code, we retain the referral attribution while the individual's account remains active.

Waitlist Data

If you join the waitlist on bridgette.com.ph, we collect your name, email address, and whether you are a patient or a provider.

3. Why We Process Your Data

We process your personal data based on one or more lawful criteria under the Data Privacy Act of 2012, including your consent where required, the performance of a contract with you, compliance with legal obligations, protection of vital interests, and our legitimate interests, as applicable. For health information, which is sensitive personal information, we rely on your consent, unless another lawful basis under Section 13 of the Data Privacy Act applies.

Specifically, we process your data to:

  • Provide and maintain the Bridgette health wallet service;

  • Allow you to record, organize, and view your health information, including laboratory results, medical history, diagnoses, and medicine and supplement logs;

  • Enable you to create and manage your account, including your use of the platform as a patient or healthcare provider;

  • Facilitate account registration and referral attribution;

  • Enable the providers you choose to view the records you share and to send you notes, which are added to your record only if you accept them;

  • Deliver notes from providers to individuals who do not yet have a Bridgette account, through a secure link;

  • Let you book with a provider through the provider’s Bridgette booking link, and let you and your providers, and any caregivers you allow, message each other, including by sending photos and files;

  • Read the laboratory result and consultation photos you choose to add to your records, using an AI service, so that you do not have to type in the values;

  • Keep records of access, consent, and disputes, to protect patients and providers and to prevent misuse of a provider’s name or license;

  • Facilitate communication between you, your caregivers, and healthcare providers participating in your care team, including allowing authorized caregivers to communicate with providers on your behalf;

  • Send you notifications you have opted into (for example, when a provider opens your share link or sends you a note, or when a note is waiting for your decision); and

  • Improve our product and fix issues.

We never sell your personal or health data to anyone. We never use your health data for advertising. Except where required or permitted by applicable law, we never share your data without your explicit consent.

4. Data Sharing and Your Control

You are always in control of who sees your health data.

We may disclose your personal data only as authorized by you, where necessary to provide the Services, or where required or permitted by applicable law.

Where personal data is disclosed, Bridgette takes appropriate measures to ensure that recipients process such information only for the intended purpose and in accordance with applicable privacy and security requirements.

Depending on how you use Bridgette, your data may be shared in the following circumstances:

  • Provider sharing: You choose which providers can access your data by generating a secure share link. You can revoke access at any time. The provider sees only the parts you select, on a view-only basis, until the link expires or you turn it off. Notes a provider writes back are added to your record only if you accept them.

  • Caregiver access: You may give a caregiver, such as a family member, access to your records. A caregiver can only view your records by default. You may allow more, such as sharing with providers you have already approved, or messaging your providers on your behalf. You can change or remove a caregiver’s access at any time. Messages a caregiver sends are clearly labeled as coming from the caregiver, not from you.

  • Bookings and messages: The provider you book with or message can see your name, your contact details, and what you send them. They cannot see your health records unless you share them.

  • AI processing: Meal entries and summaries are sent to Anthropic with personal identifiers removed. Photos you choose to add to your records are sent as described in Section 2.

  • Notes to people without a Bridgette account: When a provider sends a note to someone who does not have an account, we send only a secure link by email, as described in Section 2.

5. Data Storage and Security

We are committed to protecting the confidentiality, integrity, availability, and security of your personal data. We implement appropriate physical, organizational, and technical security measures designed to protect your personal data against accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorized disclosure, or access, in accordance with the Data Privacy Act and applicable National Privacy Commission issuances.

Your data is stored securely on Supabase infrastructure hosted in the Asia-Pacific region (Tokyo). We implement the following security measures:

  • Encryption in transit using TLS 1.2 or higher

  • Encryption at rest for all stored data

  • Row-level security ensuring users can only access their own data

  • Secure authentication with email verification and a Face ID or fingerprint lock on the app

  • Private storage buckets accessed only through signed, time-limited URLs

  • Records of who opens your share links and when, and of consent and access decisions

  • Secret keys kept on our servers and not inside the app, and replaced if we believe they may have been exposed

  • Regular security reviews

  • Emails and push notifications that never contain health information. They only tell you that something is waiting for you in Bridgette.

Access to personal data is limited to authorized personnel and service providers who require such access to perform their functions and who are subject to appropriate confidentiality and security obligations. We also maintain incident response and breach management procedures and will comply with applicable breach notification requirements under the Data Privacy Act and relevant NPC issuances.

6. International Data Transfers

Your primary data is stored in the Asia-Pacific region (Tokyo) via Supabase. Meal entries and summaries are de-identified before they are sent to Anthropic, our AI processing partner, in the United States, because no Philippine region is currently available for this service. Photos you choose to add to your records are sent as they are, with your consent, because a photo cannot be de-identified (see Section 2). Some of our other service providers, including Netlify (website and provider web application hosting) and Sentry (crash reports), are also located outside the Philippines. Cross-border transfers are covered by Data Processing Agreements with each partner and appropriate contractual, organizational, and technical safeguards. Bridgette requires its overseas service providers to implement data protection and security measures that afford a level of protection comparable to that required under the Data Privacy Act and applicable National Privacy Commission issuances, or a higher standard where required by the laws of the recipient jurisdiction. Bridgette remains accountable for your data throughout the transfer and processing lifecycle in accordance with the DPA and NPC issuances on cross-border data transfers.

7. Sub-Processors

We engage trusted third-party service providers ("sub-processors") to perform certain functions necessary to provide and support the Bridgette Services. These sub-processors process personal data only on our documented instructions and only to the extent necessary to perform the services for which they have been engaged.

We currently use the following sub-processors to deliver the Bridgette service:

  • Supabase Inc. : database, authentication, and storage (Tokyo), and delivery of account emails, one-time codes, and secure note links

  • Anthropic PBC : AI summaries of your logs, per-meal food analysis, and reading of the laboratory and consultation photos you choose to add (United States)

  • Netlify Inc. : website hosting for bridgette.com.ph and the provider web application (United States)

  • Apple Inc. and Google LLC : in-app subscription billing for patient plans

  • PayMongo : web-based subscription billing for provider plans (Xendit is being set up as an additional payment provider)

  • Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.): crash reports and app errors, without health record content (United States)

Each sub-processor is bound by a Data Processing Agreement and is required to implement appropriate contractual, organizational, and technical safeguards to protect personal data at a level comparable to that required under the Data Privacy Act and applicable National Privacy Commission issuances, or a higher standard where required by applicable law. Bridgette remains accountable for the processing of your personal data carried out by its sub-processors.

We may update this list of sub-processors from time to time as our products, services, and technology providers evolve. Any material changes will be reflected in this Privacy Notice.

8. Data Retention

We retain your personal and health data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this Privacy Notice or as otherwise required or permitted by applicable law.

Your health data is retained for as long as your account is active. If you choose to delete your account, you can do so in the app or by emailing dpo@bridgette.com.ph, and before deleting we will send a secure download link to your email address so you can download a complete export of your health data. The link requires you to verify your identity and expires after seven (7) days. Your health story belongs to you, and we will always return it to you before removing it from our systems. After your export is delivered, all data associated with your account will be permanently deleted within 30 days from production systems and within 90 days from backups. We may retain certain personal data for longer periods where required or permitted by applicable law, including for compliance with legal or regulatory obligations, audit and recordkeeping requirements, or for the establishment, exercise, or defense of legal claims.

Waitlist data collected on bridgette.com.ph is retained until you are onboarded to the product or until you ask us to delete it by emailing dpo@bridgette.com.ph, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by applicable law.

Chat messages, photos, and files are kept for as long as your account is active. Bookings are kept for as long as your account is active. Notes you decline are kept in your private archive until you delete them or close your account. Notes sent to individuals without a Bridgette account are deleted if they are not opened and saved within ninety (90) days. Records of access, consent, and disputes are kept for as long as necessary to protect patients and providers, in line with this Section.

Once the applicable retention period expires, your personal data is securely deleted, anonymized, or otherwise disposed of using appropriate measures designed to prevent unauthorized access, disclosure, or further processing.

9. Cookies and Tracking

The Bridgette mobile app does not use cookies. Our website (bridgette.com.ph) uses only essential cookies required for basic functionality. We do not use third-party advertising cookies or tracking pixels.

10. Children’s Privacy

Bridgette is not intended for individuals under eighteen (18) years of age, and minors may not create accounts. Dependent profiles for minors are planned for a later phase and will be covered by an updated Privacy Notice before they are offered.

11. Your Rights Under the Data Privacy Act

  • Right to be informed about how your data is collected and processed, including the purposes, scope, and methods of processing;

  • Right to access your personal data in our possession, including information on how such data has been processed and disclosed;

  • Right to object to the processing of your data based on legitimate interests;

  • Right to erasure or blocking of your data where it is inaccurate, incomplete, outdated, unlawfully obtained, or no longer necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, subject to applicable legal and contractual requirements;

  • Right to rectification of inaccurate or incomplete data, subject to reasonable verification;

  • Right to data portability to obtain your data in a structured format or to be transmitted to another entity;

  • Right to withdraw consent at any time, without affecting the processing carried out before the withdrawal;

  • Right to damages for any violations of your privacy rights; and

  • Right to file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission, if you believe that your data privacy rights have been violated.

To exercise any of these rights, contact our Data Protection Officer at dpo@bridgette.com.ph. We may require certain personal data for the purpose of verifying your identity or the identity of the individual making the request.

You may authorize another person to assist you in exercising your data subject rights through Bridgette, subject to applicable verification and authorization requirements. Where an Authorized Representative submits a request on your behalf, Bridgette may require evidence of the representative's authority to act for you and may take reasonable steps to verify the identity and authority of both the data subject and the Authorized Representative.
